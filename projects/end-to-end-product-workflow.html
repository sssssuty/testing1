Overview

Getting paid in US healthcare takes too many steps and too many tools.

After a patient visit, a claim has to be coded, checked, sent, tracked, corrected, and sometimes appealed before the practice gets paid. Billing teams did this across EHRs, clearinghouses, payer portals, and spreadsheets.

Youlify brings the whole cycle into one product. As the founding designer, I designed it from the first claim to the final payment.

Problem

Automation without visibility feels like a black box.

AI can do much of this work. But billing is regulated, and every mistake costs money. Teams would only hand work to AI if they could see what it did and step in at any time.

  • Fragmented toolsStaff re-entered the same data in several systems and lost track of claims between them.
  • Late signalsDenials and underpayments were often found weeks later in a report.
  • No clear ownerIt was hard to tell which claims needed a person and which were fine.

Approach

One agent for each step. One place to supervise them.

I mapped the revenue cycle with physicians and certified billers. For each step, we defined what the AI does alone, what it drafts for review, and what stays with a person.

  • Patient AccessCalculates and explains what the patient owes before check-in.
  • Documentation & CodingChecks documentation and coding, and fixes common errors.
  • Payment IntegritySpots denials and underpayments as they happen.
  • Denial ManagementFinds patterns, maps root causes, and drafts appeals for expert review.
  • Payer PolicyTracks payer rules and explains what changed.

Solution

A command center built for supervision.

  • Work queueClaims are sorted by what needs a person now. Everything else keeps moving.
  • Claim detailEvery claim has a full history from submission to payment, including what each agent did and why.
  • Review and approveAgent drafts appear next to the source documents. Users approve, edit, or reject in one step.
  • Bulk actionsFix a pattern once and apply it to every claim that shares it.

The product works on its own or with Youlify's billing service. Both use the same screens, so clients see exactly what our team sees.

Impact

Faster payments, fewer denials.

  • 51 daysShorter payment cycles
  • 84%Fewer denials
  • 90%Appeal success rate

Youlify has processed more than 3.6 million claims and managed more than $175 million in transactions. The work received the iF Design Award.
