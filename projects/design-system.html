A Design System That Lets One Designer Ship Like A Team

Shared tokens, components, and AI patterns that keep a fast-moving product consistent.

Overview Problem Approach Solution Impact

Overview

One designer, a fast team, and a product full of dense data.

As the only designer at Youlify, I could not review every screen. Engineers ship quickly, often with AI coding tools. I needed a system that makes the right design the easy choice, with or without me in the room.

Problem

Speed was creating drift.

  • Inconsistent screensThe same claim status looked different in different places. Tables behaved differently from page to page.
  • Brand and product apartMarketing and product used different colors and type.
  • New AI patternsAgents, reasoning, and review steps had no standard, so each feature invented its own.

Approach

Build from real screens, not from a library.

I started by auditing what we had shipped and kept only what repeated. The system stays small so it stays in use.

  • Tokens firstColor, type, and spacing are shared by the brand and the product.
  • Few components, done wellTables, filters, statuses, and drawers carry most of the product, so they got the most care.
  • Written for people and AIEach component has clear rules that engineers and their coding tools can both follow.

Solution

Foundations, components, and patterns for AI.

  • FoundationsIndigo-based color, a clear type scale, and spacing made for dense data.
  • Data componentsTables, filters, and charts with consistent sorting, selection, and bulk actions.
  • Status languageOne set of labels and colors for every claim state.
  • AI patternsStandard ways to show what an agent did, why it did it, and where a person needs to review.

Impact

Consistent by default.

New features now start from existing parts. Engineers can build many screens without a full mockup, and my time moves from fixing details to solving new problems.

The same tokens run the product, the website, and marketing materials.
