Overview

Medical billing is not known for good design. That was the opportunity.

Youlify is an AI billing company founded by physicians. It needed a brand that could stand next to modern fintech products and still feel at home in a clinic.

I designed the brand and the product together, so both speak with one voice.

Problem

Two audiences, one crowded category.

  • PhysiciansBusy, skeptical of vendors, and tired of billing jargon. They need to feel understood.
  • Health system leadersCareful buyers who look for proof, security, and scale.
  • The categoryMost competitors look the same: stock photos, blue gradients, and long feature lists.

Approach

Start from the promise.

The brand promise is simple: get paid for every patient you help. Every design choice supports three ideas.

  • ClarityPlain words and simple data visuals replace codes and jargon.
  • CalmGenerous space and a quiet palette lower the stress around money.
  • TrustBuilt by physicians for physicians, and shown through the real product instead of stock imagery.

Solution

A system that works from a favicon to a conference booth.

  • ColorA scrub-inspired indigo. It signals care without hospital clichés.
  • TypographyClean and structured, set for long numbers and short headlines.
  • Data visualsSimple charts turn financial detail into something readable at a glance.
  • VoiceConfident and kind. Short sentences, no jargon.

I applied the system to the website, sales materials, social campaigns, and conference booths. I also built templates so the team can make new materials without a designer.

Impact

A young company with the presence of an established one.

Sales conversations now start with a clear story. The website, the pitch deck, and the product look like one company, which matters when you ask a practice to trust you with its revenue.
