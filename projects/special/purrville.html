About Purrville

Purrville is a town where cats live like people. Anyone can bring a cat. There are no accounts.

Each cat moves into its creator's house. On weekdays, kittens go to school and grown-ups go to the office. Some days they hang out with friends after school or work. On weekends they sleep in, run errands, visit friends and sometimes take trips. At night they go to bed when people do.

You can watch the houses and the office. School, commutes, cafés and trips happen out of sight. Before a cat leaves home it writes a note on the fridge, and takes it down when it gets back.

The clock follows your own time by default. Use 60× or 720× to fast-forward, and Live to come back.

Where your cats live

Cats and photos are saved in this browser only. To move cats to a friend's town, use Export town file, send the file, and have them choose Import cats from a file.

Open source

The whole site is this one HTML file, MIT licensed. Save it, change it, host it on any static host. To give everyone one shared town, connect the Store object in the source to a backend.
