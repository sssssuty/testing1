Overview

Billing data is rich. Getting an answer from it was slow.

Youlify runs the revenue cycle for healthcare practices. Every claim, denial, and payment lives in the system. But when a practice owner asked a simple question, like why collections dropped last month, someone still had to export a report and build a chart by hand.

I designed a conversation where users ask a question and get three things back: a short answer, a chart, and the claims behind the number.

Problem

Dashboards only answer the questions we predicted.

Working with practice owners, billing managers, and our own billing team, three problems kept coming up.

  • Fixed dashboardsEach new question needed a new report. Most questions were asked once and never again.
  • Not everyone is an analystPhysicians know their practice, not pivot tables. They wanted answers, not tools.
  • Money needs proofA wrong number is worse than no number. Users would not trust an AI answer they could not check.

Approach

Answer first. Chart second. Proof third.

Every response follows the same order, so users always know where to look.

  • AnswerOne or two sentences that state the result in plain words.
  • ChartThe AI picks the chart type from the question. Trends get a line. Comparisons get bars. A single number gets a stat.
  • ProofThe filters, date range, and claims used. One click opens the full list.

I kept the chart set small on purpose. A few chart types cover most billing questions, and a small set is easier to keep correct and consistent.

Solution

A conversation that leads to action.

  • Suggested questionsThe empty state offers questions based on the user's role and recent activity, so no one starts from a blank box.
  • Follow-upsUsers refine the same chart by asking: "only Medicare" or "compare to last quarter". The chart updates in place.
  • Drill downClick any bar or point to see the claims behind it.
  • Pin to dashboardSave a useful answer as a live tile. The dashboard becomes personal over time.
  • Hand off to an agentWhen an answer shows a problem, the user can act on it. For example, send a group of denied claims to the appeals agent.

Impact

From waiting for a report to asking a question.

Questions that used to need a custom report now take one message. Our billing team uses the same tool to prepare client reviews, and practice owners can check their numbers without waiting for us.
